Tangled by Touch

East Asia has an ancient story that describes an invisible red thread being draped between two individuals destined to meet and be together for the rest of their lives on earth.

The thread in this piece is not about the importance of connections between people. Instead, it is a sort of journal record of what you are doing. Your cursor functions as a needle, going through other threads and tying knots in them with each connection. The more you move around on your screen the more knots you make, and the more impossible it becomes to move at all.

What is the point at which a connection ceases to be meaningful and begins to be restrictive?

How to play

The user starts by holding the mouse to begin. Then the user slowly moves the cursor around on the screen to try to find the other person. The user then continues to move the cursor around to see what happens to the two threads. The user can then end the piece by either remaining still or holding the mouse.

more movement → more traces → more entanglement → less freedom
